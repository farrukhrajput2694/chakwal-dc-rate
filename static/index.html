Heads-up on urban coverage. The portal lists Tehsil Chakwal's urban property areas — 2 towns, 10 revenue circles, 118 property areas — but every one of those 118 reports no Khasra number, no Square number, and no land classification. So the urban chain stops at the Property Area: the Location dropdown stays empty and there is no narrower parcel to pick. That is a gap in the portal's own published data, not a fault in this tool. Rural data is complete and unaffected — all 226 mouzas publish classifications, locations and Khasra numbers.
